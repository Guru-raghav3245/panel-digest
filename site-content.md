## Why Panel Digest Exists

A newspaper is built for someone who already knows how to read one — dense columns, no visual hierarchy, a wall of text per story. That's exactly what makes current affairs feel like a chore to a student, not a habit. Panel Digest takes the same story a newspaper would run and draws it instead: one news event, told in six panels, in whatever art style suits that story best.

We're not making current affairs *easier* — we're making it something a student actually wants to open. Each issue is drawn fresh, so reading issue after issue never feels like filling in the same template with new headlines. And because understanding isn't the same as remembering, every issue ships with a companion quiz that turns the story back into a set of questions, so the habit sticks instead of scrolling past.

## Catalog

Every issue takes one news story and runs it twice: once as a six-panel explainer, once as a companion revision quiz. We don't reskin a house palette onto the art — each issue keeps the look it was drawn in, so the catalog looks like a shelf of different books, not one newspaper template filled in six times.

## Why We Bet On This Format

We looked for a published study on comic strips and Olympiad-style GK exams specifically and didn't find one yet — that gap is part of why we're building the catalog. Instead, here is the closest peer-reviewed evidence: vocabulary density, learning-motivation gains, and pre/post knowledge-test scores from comic-based instruction, the same mechanics our explainer-plus-quiz format leans on.

*Three studies, three subjects, one pattern: comics move the number that matters.*

### Vocabulary
### Comics Carry More Rare Words Than Children's Books

Rare words per 1,000 running words — comics sit closer to adult books than to the children's-book shelf they're often filed next to, which is the raw material a vocabulary-building quiz needs. It's also the quiet reason a comic can stand in for a newspaper rather than a picture book: the vocabulary load is already adult-grade.

| Source | Rare words / 1,000 |
|---|---|
| Comic books | 53.5 |
| Adult books | 52.7 |
| Children's books | 30.9 |
| Expert witness testimony | 28.4 |
| Adult conversation | 17.3 |

Source: Hayes, D. P. & Ahrens, M. (1988). Vocabulary simplification for children: a special case of "motherese." *Journal of Child Language*, 15(2). · Cambridge Core

### Attention & motivation
### Engagement Climbs When The Lesson Is A Comic

A classroom trial swapped a text handout for a social-studies digital comic. Self-reported learning motivation rose, and the share of students who actually met the lesson's learning targets went from a small minority to nearly all of them.

| Metric | Before comic | After comic |
|---|---|---|
| Learning motivation | 71.8% | 81.9% |
| Met learning outcomes | 11.1% | 96.3% |

Source: Effect of a social-values-based digital comic on motivation and learning outcomes, Class X, MA Al Islam Telaga Biru. *World Journal of Advanced Research and Reviews*. · WJARR

### Knowledge-check scores
### Scores Nearly Double After A Comic-Based Module

A 50-item multiple-choice test — the same format as most GK and Olympiad rounds — given before and after a four-week comic-based module. This is physics, not current affairs, but the test design is the closest published match to a quiz-style knowledge check.

| Test | Mean score (out of 50) |
|---|---|
| Pre-test | 16.3 |
| Post-test | 29.2 |

*n = 68, whiskers show ±1 SD*

Source: Badeo, J. M. O. & Ong Kian Koc, B. C. U. (2021). Use of comic-based learning module in physics in enhancing students' achievement and motivation. *Science Education International*, 32(2), 131–136. · ERIC

## One Plan, Built For Schools

Panel Digest is sold to schools as a single site license — not to individual students or parents, and not per seat. One subscription covers every student the school enrolls, and replaces the newspaper-in-the-classroom slot rather than sitting alongside it.

### ₹5,000 per school / month

**Schools only.** Panel Digest accounts are issued to the school and distributed to students by their teachers — we don't sell direct-to-student or direct-to-parent plans.

- **The full comic catalog** — Every issue and its revision quiz, unlocked for every enrolled student as new issues ship.
- **The student app** — One login that works on the school's devices and at home — reading and quizzes aren't locked to the classroom.
- **Extreme analytics** — The school-wide version of our Analytics page: vocabulary growth, engagement, and quiz-score trends broken down by class and by student, not just in aggregate.
- **Reader rewards, included** — Points and the redeem shop ship with every plan at no extra cost.
